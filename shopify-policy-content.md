# Shipping & Delivery Policy — Baibhav Collections

At **Baibhav Collections**, we are committed to delivering your orders with uncompromising speed, secure tamper-proof packaging, and total transparency right to your doorstep.

---

### 1. What is Baibhav Collections Shipping Policy?
Baibhav Collections ships your purchased items—including our trending range of **mens swimming shorts**, resort wear, and designer apparel—in the fastest time possible. Every order is carefully checked and dispatched from our fulfillment facility within **24 to 48 hours**. 

Please verify your delivery location by entering your PIN code at our checkout page before placing your order.

### 2. Payment Modes, Discounts & COD Handling Fees
We offer safe digital payments as well as Cash on Delivery (COD):
* **Prepaid Orders (UPI / Debit & Credit Card / NetBanking):**
  * **Flat 5% Extra Instant Discount** applied automatically at checkout.
  * **Zero COD Handling Charge** (Save ₹49).
  * Priority processing and express dispatch.
* **Cash on Delivery (COD):**
  * A nominal handling fee of **₹49** is applied to cover courier cash logistics.
  * Pay comfortably via cash or courier UPI QR code upon receiving your order.
* **Complimentary Free Shipping:** All orders above **₹999** qualify for **Free Shipping** across India!

### 3. How Can I Check the Status of My Order?
You can monitor your order journey at every milestone:
1. **Account Dashboard:** Sign in to your account and click **My Account > Order History** to view active dispatch status.
2. **SMS & WhatsApp Updates:** You will receive automated SMS and WhatsApp alerts containing live tracking links when your order is dispatched and out for delivery.
3. **Dispatch Email:** An email with your Air Waybill (AWB) number and direct courier tracking link is sent upon dispatch.

### 4. How Can I Check If Baibhav Collections Delivers to My PIN Code?
Our checkout page features an instant PIN code verification tool. Simply enter your 6-digit postal code. If the checkout allows you to proceed, delivery is actively serviceable at your location.

### 5. If Delivery is Not Available at My Address, What Should I Do?
We are actively expanding our courier network across India. If your PIN code is not currently serviceable:
* Please check back in a few days as new postal routes are activated weekly.
* You can provide an alternative serviceable address (such as an office or nearby location).
* Contact our customer concierge at **support@baibhavcollections.com** — we can arrange special delivery via India Post Speed Post.

### 6. Delivery Timelines & Courier Partners
All orders placed on Baibhav Collections are shipped through India's premier courier partners, including **Blue Dart, Delhivery, DTDC, Xpressbees, Shadowfax, and India Post**.

* **Tier 1 Metro Cities:** 2 – 4 Business Days
* **Tier 2 & State Capitals:** 3 – 5 Business Days
* **Rest of India & Rural Towns:** 4 – 7 Business Days
* **Special Regions (NE, J&K):** 5 – 8 Business Days

*Note on Unforeseen Delays:* While 98%+ deliveries arrive on schedule, delays may occasionally occur due to adverse weather, festival volume, public transport restrictions, incorrect contact numbers, or incomplete addresses.

### 7. Damaged, Missing, or Tampered Parcels
* All orders are dispatched in tamper-evident security packaging.
* If the outer package appears torn, previously opened, or resealed with unbranded tape, **please refuse delivery** from the courier agent.
* For quick claim resolution, we strongly recommend recording a brief unboxing video upon delivery.
* Any issue or transit damage must be reported to our support team within **48 hours** of receiving the parcel.
