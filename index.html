1. What is Baibhav Collections Shipping Policy?

At Baibhav Collections, we endeavor to ship products ordered by you in the fastest turnaround time possible and deliver them safely to your doorstep in luxury, tamper-evident packaging.

We process all orders within 24 to 48 working hours after verification. All shipments are managed through India's premier courier networks with end-to-end tracking from dispatch until final handover.

Important Notice on Pin Code Verification: Please check the delivery availability at your PIN Code before placing an order. If our automated checkout permits completion, your location is fully serviceable.

5. Serviceability & What If My Address Is Not Serviceable?

How can I check if Baibhav Collections delivers at my PIN Code?
Our checkout page has a dedicated PIN code verification tool. Please enter your 6-digit postal code there. If the checkout proceeds, delivery is fully supported at your location.

If delivery is not available at my address, what should I do?
We continuously partner with additional regional courier hubs to expand our delivery coverage. If your PIN code is currently unserviceable:

  • Please check back in a few days as new PIN codes are added every week.
  • You may provide an alternative shipping address (such as an office address, friend, or relative residing in a neighboring serviceable locality).
  • Contact our customer support team directly at support@baibhavcollections.com — we can often arrange custom dispatch via India Post Speed Post for remote rural locations.

7. Package Inspection, Damaged or Tampered Shipments

Every piece at Baibhav Collections undergoes a rigorous multi-stage quality check and is sealed in our branded, tamper-evident outer polybag or security box.

  • Do Not Accept Damaged Outer Packaging: If the parcel appears clearly opened, torn, or taped with generic unbranded tape, please refuse acceptance from the delivery person and mention "Refused due to tampered package" on the delivery sheet.
  • Unboxing Video: We strongly advise recording a 360-degree unboxing video right from opening the outer sealed packet. This ensures immediate replacement or refund in the rare event of missing or transit-damaged items.
  • 48-Hour Reporting Window: Any discrepancies or transit damage must be notified to us within 48 hours of delivery receipt.
